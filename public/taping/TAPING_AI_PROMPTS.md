## Shared clinical language (read once)

**Anchors** = circular strips that do not cross the joint; they fix the ends of active strips.

**Active strips (tiras activas)** = strips that cross the joint in a direction that **limits a specific motion**:
- To **limit inversion** (supination / rolling inward): tension runs from **medial → plantar → lateral**, pulling the foot toward eversion / locking the lateral complex.
- To **limit eversion** (pronation / rolling outward): tension runs from **lateral → plantar → medial**, pulling the foot toward inversion / locking the medial complex.
- Stirrups, heel locks, and figure-8s are the main active constructions at the ankle.

Always show the foot held in the **closed-pack / protected position** named in each prompt while active strips are applied (usually slight dorsiflexion + slight eversion for lateral ankle protection).

0) Prep (1–2 s): Show clean dry skin. Clinician tears tape ends neatly. Optional: briefly show skin check around malleoli — no underwrap in this version.

1) Proximal anchor (tiras de anclaje proximales): Apply TWO overlapping circular strips around the lower third of the shin, just ABOVE both malleoli. Strips meet on the anterior shin, snug but not tourniquet-tight. Do NOT cross the ankle joint. Smooth firmly.

2) Distal anchor (tiras de anclaje distales): Apply TWO overlapping circular strips around the midfoot over the metatarsal shafts, leaving ALL toes free and the first MTP joint free enough to move slightly. Smooth firmly.

3) Active stirrups to LIMIT INVERSION (tiras en estribo / U-strips — CRITICAL DETAIL):
   - Tear a vertical strip long enough to go from proximal anchor → under heel → back to proximal anchor.
   - Start on the MEDIAL side of the proximal anchor (near medial malleolus).
   - With the foot held in slight dorsiflexion + slight eversion, pull the tape DOWN past the medial malleolus, UNDER the heel (calcaneus), then UP the LATERAL side past the lateral malleolus, ending on the proximal anchor.
   - Apply WITH CLEAR TENSION on the lateral upward pass — this active pull resists inversion.
   - Apply a SECOND and THIRD stirrup overlapping the first by ~50%, each slightly more anterior than the last, same medial→plantar→lateral direction. Smooth each one.

4) Heel locks to reinforce inversion control (tiras de cierre de talón):
   - Start on the anterior proximal anchor, angle down toward the medial heel, wrap under the heel, then lock up around the lateral heel/Achilles and return to the anterior shin.
   - Repeat a second heel lock from the opposite start so both sides of the calcaneus are cupped. Keep foot in protected position.

5) Figure-8 active passes (figura en 8 — clearly visible X on the dorsum):
   - FIRST figure-8: start on the MEDIAL distal/proximal junction near medial malleolus → down under the plantar arch → up over the LATERAL malleolus → behind Achilles → return medially, forming one clear loop of the 8.
   - SECOND figure-8: continue / reverse so the strip crosses the FIRST on the DORSUM of the foot-ankle, creating a clear “X”. Direction continues to support anti-inversion (medial start, lateral reinforcement).
   - Optional third light figure-8 for coverage if time allows within 20 s.

6) Closing anchors: re-apply one proximal circular strip and one distal circular strip over the free ends to lock everything. Smooth tape flat over both malleoli and the dorsum. Final hold 1–2 seconds on the finished job: dense white figure-8 + stirrups, toes free, foot still slightly dorsiflexed/everted, visibly supported against inversion.

Prioritize readable tape PATH and HAND PLACEMENT over speed. Educational physiotherapy demonstration video, anatomically accurate, calm professional clinician and patient, camera steady, 20 seconds.

## Ankle cheat-sheet (tiras activas)

**Limitar INVERSIÓN**
- Posición: ligera dorsiflexión + ligera eversión
- Estribos: medial → bajo el talón → lateral (tensión al subir por lateral)
- Figura en 8: refuerza el complejo lateral; X visible en el dorso
- Heel locks: “abrazan” el calcáneo

**Limitar EVERSIÓN**
- Posición: ligera dorsiflexión + ligera inversión
- Estribos: lateral → bajo el talón → medial (tensión al subir por medial)
- Figura en 8 y heel locks en sentido inverso / énfasis medial

Never apply circumferential tape as a tourniquet. Toes must stay pink and movable. Leave fingernails/toenails visible for perfusion check when relevant.
